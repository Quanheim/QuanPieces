# Changelog

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-26

- Added Quan's Street Lights, both in the QuanPieces tab and compatible with NoSmokeStayLit's timer:
  - **Brazier Street Light**: a coal brazier on a wooden post. Costs 5 Bronze, 2 Coal, 1 Chain and 4 Wood at a
    Forge. Stays lit in rain under its arm, but burns low in strong wind.
  - **Dvergr Street Light**: a Dvergr lantern on a Grausten and Ashwood post. Costs 3 Copper, 1 Dvergr Lantern,
    1 Chain, 6 Grausten and 4 Ashwood at a Black Forge. Burns wisps, ignores the weather, lights a wide area and
    keeps you warm up to 50 m away.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-25

- Initial release: Quan's Chimney Flue, a 2x2 stone louvred flue that keeps rain off a fire while letting its
  smoke through. Costs 5 Stone at a Workbench and snaps and tiles with itself for wider chimneys.
