# Changelog

## 1.5.0 - unreleased

- New pieces: Quan's Wood Wall, Quan's Wood Wall Half and Quan's Wood Wall 1x1. The vanilla wood walls with only
  their vertical planks, no beams across the back, at vanilla's cost.
- New pieces: Quan's Wood Wall 2x3 (3 Wood) and Quan's Wood Wall 1x3 (2 Wood). Plank walls 3 m tall, with the wood
  grain carried up the planks rather than stretched. Like the walls above, they have no beams.
- New pieces: Quan's Triangular Wood Floor 1x1 (1) and (2), 1 Wood each. A 1x1 wood floor cut corner to corner,
  for small gaps or a neat 45° end to a 1x1 shelf. Mirror images of each other.
- New piece: Quan's Bevelled Shelf Edge, 1 Wood. The same triangle with its planks along the long edge, for a
  bevelled end to a 1x1 shelf.
- New pieces: Quan's Item Stand, for the wall and for the floor. Vanilla's item stands with only the nub, no
  backplate or underplate, so the item sits right against the wall or on the floor. Same cost as vanilla's.

## 1.4.0 - 2026-11-07

- New piece: Quan's Floor Heater (5 Stone, Workbench), built switched off. While it's on, Quan's Chicken Coop
  Floors within 10 m are warm and eggs on them hatch; turn it off to collect eggs instead. It needs no fuel and
  doesn't mind rain or open sky.
- Quan's Chicken Coop Floor is now warm only while a floor heater that's on is within 10 m. Existing coops need a
  heater to keep hatching eggs.
- Removed Quan's Unheated Chicken Coop Floor (added in 1.3.0); the heater does its job. Any that were placed
  disappear.

## 1.3.0 - 2026-11-07

- New piece: Quan's Unheated Chicken Coop Floor. The same coop floor without the warmth, so eggs laid on it
  don't hatch and can be collected.

## 1.2.2 - 2026-10-31

- Quan's Chimney Flue can now be walked on like a grate. Smoke still rises straight through it.

## 1.2.1 - 2026-10-31

- Planters now accept everything you plant with the cultivator, not only crop and tree seedlings: for example
  PlantEverything's mushrooms, berry bushes and flowers, which previously couldn't be placed in them.
- Aiming the cultivator at a planter's soil no longer tints the whole planter blue; aiming at the rim still does.
- Removing a planter now clears everything in it, not just seedlings: anything ready to pick drops its harvest,
  and PlantEverything's mushrooms and bushes give back what PlantEverything gives when you remove them by hand.
  Previously they were left floating in the air. Fully grown crops now drop their harvest too, rather than
  falling to the ground unpicked.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-10-30

- Added Quan's Planters, raised beds of cultivated soil in a wooden frame, in the QuanPieces tab:
  - **Planter**: 1x2, costs 10 Wood at a Workbench.
  - **Large Planter**: 1x4, costs 16 Wood at a Workbench.
- Anything you can plant with the cultivator grows in them, in any biome, under a roof, with no heat or cold
  damage, packed in as tightly as you like. Removing a planter gives back the seeds of anything still growing.
- Added Quan's Chicken Coop, three 2x2 pieces in the QuanPieces tab, each costing 2 Wood at a Workbench:
  - **Chicken Coop Floor**: a wooden floor with low thatch, so chicks stand tall. It gives off warmth, so eggs
    laid on it hatch.
  - **Chicken Coop Wall**: wooden bars you can see and reach through to pick up eggs, but animals can't pass.
  - **Chicken Coop Door**: a 2x2 gate of wooden bars with an iron latch.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-26

- Added Quan's Street Lights, both in the QuanPieces tab and compatible with NoSmokeStayLit's timer:
  - **Brazier Street Light**: a coal brazier on a wooden post. Costs 5 Bronze, 2 Coal, 1 Chain and 4 Wood at a
    Forge. Stays lit in rain under its arm, but burns low in strong wind.
  - **Dvergr Street Light**: a Dvergr lantern on a Grausten and Ashwood post. Costs 3 Copper, 1 Dvergr Lantern,
    1 Chain, 6 Grausten and 4 Ashwood at a Black Forge. Burns wisps, ignores the weather, lights a wide area and
    keeps you warm up to 50 m away.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-25

- Initial release: Quan's Chimney Flue, a 2x2 stone louvred flue that keeps rain off a fire while letting its
  smoke through. Costs 5 Stone at a Workbench and snaps and tiles with itself for wider chimneys.
